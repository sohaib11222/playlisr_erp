GRADING & CONDITION (education)
We grade to Goldmine standards. Quick guide: Mint (M) = perfect/sealed; Near Mint (NM) = looks and plays like new; Very Good Plus (VG+) = light signs of play/handling, still sounds great; Very Good (VG) = more visible wear and some surface noise; Good/Good Plus (G/G+) = plays through but with noticeable wear/noise, a collector/filler grade. Some light surface noise is normal on used records, especially below VG+ — used records are graded carefully but aren't guaranteed dead silent, which is why every store has a listening station to test before you buy. Full guide: https://www.nivessa.com/guides/vinyl-grading. Used records are final sale; sealed/unopened items can be returned within 30 days.

We buy every day — no appointment (walk in during open hours; only call ahead for 200+ records or an entire estate). We're a "maximalist" buyer: we buy full collections and don't cherry-pick, so you don't get stuck with the leftovers. We buy far more than records: vinyl, CDs, cassettes, DVDs, Blu-ray, VHS, 8-tracks; turntables, receivers, speakers, amps and other stereo/audio gear; instruments and DJ gear; posters, music books, band t-shirts, and collectibles.
Big collections: we make house calls across the LA area for large lots — got a basement or storage unit full? Call or text (213) 676-2645 to set up a pickup. We also offer free junk removal for estates and cleanouts.
How we price: every offer is based on live Discogs sales data, real sell-through, and the actual condition, rarity, and demand of your items — no flat-rate price sheets. Cash offers generally run ~15–35% of market value depending on grade and demand (e.g. a $100 record is roughly $15–35 cash; a $500 record roughly $75–175). You can take cash, or choose store credit worth 50% more than the cash offer — credit is the best way to maximize your collection's value. Photos help us quote faster — text them to (213) 676-2645 or email hello@nivessa.com. For ballpark values first, try https://www.nivessa.com/vinyl-worth or the seller flow at https://www.nivessa.com/buy-sell-trade.
Nivessa vs Discogs: selling to us is instant and local — cash (or 50% more in store credit) the same day, with no listing, fees, shipping, or waiting; we aim to be competitive with online prices.
What we generally won't buy: water-damaged or moldy records, badly warped or scratched unplayable vinyl, and beat-up common mainstream LPs. Selling questions: hello@nivessa.com.
